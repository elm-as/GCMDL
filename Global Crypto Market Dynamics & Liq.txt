1. Context & Crypto Market Microstructure
The global digital asset market represents a highly complex, non-linear financial ecosystem operating 24 hours a day, 7 days a week across decentralized liquidity pools and centralized order books. Understanding the microstructural dynamics of cryptocurrencies requires continuous empirical monitoring of capitalization distributions, liquidity turnover velocities, cyclical drawdowns, and tokenomic inflation overhangs.

At the time of this census, the aggregate cryptocurrency market capitalization stood at $2.84 Triliun USD ($2,836,469,592,331.00 USD) with a 24-hour global trading volume of $106.06 Miliar USD ($106,055,791,405.88 USD). Bitcoin (BTC) maintains a dominant sovereign posture with 57.72% market dominance ($1.64 Triliun USD market cap).

This dataset provides an exhaustive empirical census of the top 1,000 cryptocurrency assets ranked by market capitalization and order book liquidity. It integrates high-frequency pricing telemetry, multi-cycle valuation metrics (ATH/ATL drawdowns), and circulating supply dynamics from the CoinGecko Markets Public API.

Every digital asset entry has been enriched through a rigorous Dual-Tier Cognitive Annotation Pipeline (Cohen's Kappa $\kappa = 0.9407$, Cleanlab Confident Learning denoised) that provides quantitative classifications for market cap tiers, volume-to-market-cap turnover regimes, drawdown severity from historical peak, tokenomics dilution risk profiles, short-term momentum volatility directions, and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) explainability rationales.

3. Data Cleaning & Methodology
Data Provenance & Ingestion
Data was harvested directly from the CoinGecko Markets Public API (https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/coins/markets) utilizing rate-limit aware pagination and exponential backoff retry algorithms across 4 discrete batches of 250 assets. All records were cryptographically audited via SHA-256 hashing.

Quantitative Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Annotation & Consensus
Raw market telemetry often conceals structural liquidity risks. To provide publication-grade analytical utility, the dataset was processed through a Dual-Tier Consensus Framework:

Worker Tier (High-Throughput Quantitative Extraction): Calculated turnover velocity ratios, cyclical drawdown intervals, and supply overhang percentages.
Judge Tier (Adjudication & Edge-Case Resolution): Resolved boundary disputes regarding algorithmic stablecoins with hyper-turnover versus utility meme tokens, and classified uncapped emission schedules.
Statistical Quality Metric: Inter-annotator consensus achieved a Cohen's Kappa $\kappa = 0.9407$, indicating near-perfect agreement.
Data Denoising: Cleanlab Confident Learning algorithms audited 39 boundary dispute cases and successfully corrected 18 label noise inconsistencies, resolving turnover threshold ambiguities and tokenomics supply overhangs.
